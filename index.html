Australia’s International Student Map

Where international students come from, where they study, and how the pattern is changing.

1. The big picture

International education is one of Australia’s major global connections. This chart uses January snapshots so each year is compared at the same point in the calendar.

2. Where students come from

A small number of source countries contribute a large share of Australia’s international students.

3. Where they study in Australia

Raw student numbers and per-capita rates tell different stories about the geographic concentration of international students.

4. What students study

Different education sectors have grown and changed at different speeds. Click the sector legend in the area chart to highlight a sector.

The final section compares recent change by state and shows how each state’s national share changed between 2019 and 2026.
